EVDriveSound V9.1 – Continuous Sound Test

The previous test still contained staged time blocks. Those stages could be heard as thresholds even though the sound engine itself had no gears.

V9.1 removes the staged test completely.

The test now uses:
- one continuous smoothstep time curve
- one continuous sine speed envelope
- analytical acceleration from the derivative of that curve
- continuous low-pass smoothing
- 20 ms updates
- no if/else acceleration stages
- no gear thresholds
- no shift events
- no frequency resets

The only endpoint condition is stopping the completed test at 9 seconds.
