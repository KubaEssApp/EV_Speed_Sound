EVDriveSound V9 Continuous Sound Engine

This build was regenerated from scratch specifically to remove all gear-shift behavior.

Guaranteed by code structure:
- No gear variable
- No gear thresholds
- No shift events
- No shift sound
- No RPM reset
- No discrete frequency jumps
- No staged transmission model

All sound pitch is calculated continuously from:
1. Tesla GPS speed
2. Smoothed acceleration / deceleration

Frequency changes use continuous setTargetAtTime() smoothing only.
